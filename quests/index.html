Deer Isle: The Main Line

  • Ch0: An Older Story
    Learn that the island has an older history than STAG's labs.
    Underground standing
  • Ch1: The Prison Card
    Search Alcatraz for a S.T.A.G. Level 5 Laboratory Keycard.
    Survivors + Underground standing
  • Ch2: The Crater
    Gear up in NBC gear, climb the Crater tower, retrieve Sample X65.
    Waldo + Underground standing
  • Ch3: The Mountain
    Open the KMUC entrance, retrieve the Skull Staff, discover the Underground.
    Underground payout + rank advance
  • Ch4: The Temple
    Enter the swamp Temple at night, dive the flooded maze, craft Thor's Hammer.
    Survivors + Underground standing, Mjölnir
  • Ch5: The Cold Chamber
    Earn a bear-fur suit, break into the Frozen Crypt, light the Skull Staff at the Eternal Flame.
    Huntsman's + Underground standing, lit Staff
  • Ch6: The Echo
    Return to Alcatraz for a second Laboratory Keycard, cross into Facility III's echo, end the extraction cycle at A12.
    Major Underground payout + title
  • Epilogue: The Stranger
    The Stranger returns to close the circle.
    Story closure
