2026-10-04

Server update

🏅 Notoriety & Factions

  • Faction notoriety now has proper ranks. Check your standing in the Notoriety tab (Pause/Break, or the tab on your map screen)
  • Survivors of Deer Isle, Waldo Military Base, Groot Hill and the Huntsman's Clan all have ladders to climb
  • Rank achievements unlock as you rise (some are hidden until you earn them!)

🦖 Wildlife & Creatures

  • Dinosaurs are back, and they're no accident. They're the remnants of STAG research, and they've taken over the islands off the east side of the map. Raptors, stegos, trikes, brachs, T-Rex and more roam those islands. Cross the water at your own risk 🦕
  • Woolly mammoths roam the north
  • Tigers and giant spiders stalk the southwest jungle
  • Apes have moved into the hills, and sharks patrol the southwest coast
  • Sell your dino meat to Niki at the Huntsman

🧟 Infected

  • Bikers have taken over Portland. Watch the streets around the bike shop
  • Keep an eye out for glowing-eyed infected mixed in with the regular hordes
  • Killer Clowns may drop some very fashionable clown gear 🤡
  • Party-goers might be carrying a few… party favors

🪖 Quests & Missions

  • Sergeant Steele's "What They Left Behind" has been fixed and expanded: the cache now has its supplies, and Steele needs an escort home
  • New King of the Hill hardpoints are up
  • Several quest fixes so completions save properly

🏠 Building & Bases

  • Portable houses are now easier to place: they sit level on the ground instead of tilting with the slope
  • Anyone can now raise a plot pole flag (only the owner can lower it)
  • Groot Hill got a makeover: the traders have moved into new spots, but all the familiar faces are still there. Take a walk and say hi to your old friends 👋

🎒 Gear & Loot

  • Keycard strongrooms and building airdrops have a couple of new rare finds to discover

As always, report bugs in #bug-reports, and thanks for playing! ❤️
