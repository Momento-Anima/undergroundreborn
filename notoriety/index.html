The system

Notoriety

Every job you finish gets you noticed, by the island and by the people you did it for. Notoriety is the record of that. It never locks you out of anything, death doesn't take it, and some of the people keeping score haven't introduced themselves yet.

How it works

Every quest pays notoriety twice. Once with the whole island, the Survivors of Deer Isle, and once with the faction you did the work for, if there was one. Do a run for the bikers and the Sons of Deer Isle remember it. Do STAG's dirty work and STAG takes an interest, which is not the same thing as liking you.

Killing counts too. Dangerous animals, infected and the AI you fight all leave a mark. The bigger the thing you put down, the more people hear about it.

Climb the ladder, get a rank. Each faction has its own ranks. Crossing into a new one fires an achievement everybody on the server can see, and the rank carries a title.

It restricts nothing. There's no gear you can't use and nothing you're blocked from buying. What it may do is change the price: some traders deal more generously with a name they respect, and a few keep their better stock behind a door you have to earn.

Dying takes your gear and nothing else. Notoriety you've earned stays earned. Track it in your journal, on the Notoriety tab.

Not everyone announces themselves. Some factions earn notoriety from you before you know they exist. When you're told that someone took notice, that's who. The story will introduce you eventually.

The ladders

Each faction has its own ladder, from where you start to the top. The two island-wide ladders (the Survivors and STAG) are the longest.

Survivors of Deer Isle everyone on the island

  1. StartUnknown
  2. Rank 1Seen Around
  3. Rank 2Familiar Face
  4. Rank 3Known Name
  5. Rank 4Local Legend
  6. Rank 5Island Legend
  7. Rank 6Deer Isle's Own

It can go the other way. Below zero you're Talked About, then Bad News, Outcast, Marked and, at the bottom, Pariah.

Getting noticed

Start by talking to people. Every settlement has someone with work to do, and the jobs add up. Some say an underground network is quietly working to undo what STAG did to the island, and that it notices the people who help. Jobs that come around again keep your name climbing between the bigger stories.
