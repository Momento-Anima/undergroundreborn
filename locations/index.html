Deer Isle

Trader hubs on Deer Isle

The places you will keep coming back to. Each hub is a place to trade, restock and meet other survivors. See them on the map.

Safe zone

Groot's Hill

The first trading post you reach, and where your first series of quests begins. Coffee and food, medical supplies, weapons and ammunition, clothing and armour.

Find it on the map
Safe zone

Waldoboro

A military trading post, and where you go to buy helicopters. It is also a quest hub for some of the more dangerous missions.

Find it on the map
Safe zone

Ship Hole Harbor

Where you go to buy boats and learn how to fish. Tackle, hunting gear, boat parts, medical supplies, and even a barber.

Find it on the map
Safe zone

Sons of Deer Isle

Where you buy motorcycles, along with the parts and electronics to keep them running.

Find it on the map

The Grey Market

An in-between market. It sells things regular stores don't, but nothing so far off the beaten path as to be taboo.

Find it on the map
Safe zone

The Collectibles Trader

A purpose-built trading hall for collectors. Bring your finds, and your STAG gear, and cash them in.

Find it on the map
