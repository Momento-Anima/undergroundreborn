Missions

Our missions are our own: a custom mission engine runs a rotating set of events across Deer Isle. Hold the hill in King of the Hill, push through the multi-step Proving Grounds, hunt the bears, clear a horde, take down a VIP or raid a guarded camp, with a loot chest for whoever finishes. Rewards scale with how many players are on the server, and completing missions earns you notoriety. We keep fewer missions running at once on purpose, so a marker on your map means something.

Finds

Loot

Loot on Deer Isle is hand-built. We write our own loot tables instead of leaving it to the defaults: missions, keycard strongrooms and many loot chests all pay out from our own tiered lists. You find what you need out in the world: nails, saws and hammers are no longer sold by traders, so you take them from missions, keyrooms, King of the Hill and airdrops.

Stash

Virtual Storage

Virtual Storage protects your supplies, and it is built for our server: store gear in containers within your territory and come back later to retrieve it. Items won't despawn or decay while stored.

Everything else

On the island

Fight when you choose to

The island is PvE. Step into a marked PvP zone when you want a fight, and out again when you don't. Nobody can hunt you outside the lines. See the zones.

A better place to wake up

You start with three souls. Earn more by taking the fight to others, and spend them on a better respawn. With none left, the prison is always free.

Dinosaurs are back

They're the remnants of STAG research, and they've taken over the islands off the east side of the map: raptors, stegos, trikes, brachs, T-Rex and more. Elsewhere, woolly mammoths roam the north, tigers and giant spiders stalk the southwest jungle, and sharks patrol the southwest coast.

The best loot is behind a locked door

Keycard-locked rooms hold the good stuff, and getting in means going through whoever else wants it.

Park it, key it, find it again

Cars, boats and helicopters all use one garage system, with a fresh key every time you take one out.

A real economy

Safe-zone trader hubs at Groot's Hill, Waldoboro, Ship Hole Harbor and the Sons of Deer Isle, plus the Collectibles trader and a Grey Market. Earn it, spend it, and find the people who buy what you loot.
