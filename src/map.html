Deer Isle

Where you can get shot

The island is PvE except inside the red circles. Blue is a safe zone with traders. Yellow needs a gas mask. Orange is dinosaur country, drawn broadly. Hover or tap anything for its name.

North is up. The outline is drawn from the server's own zone data, so it's a sketch, not a satellite view: use the in-game map or the DZSA launcher's map for roads and buildings.

PvP zones

Leaving a PvP zone starts a 60-second timer. Stepping out and back in to get an advantage counts as zone strafing and is punished. Damage is disabled across the line, in both directions.
