On the island

Missions and events

The island is never quiet for long. Here is what turns up on your map, what each one asks of you, and what is waiting at the end.

PvP

King of the Hill

A flag goes up at one of the island's strongholds. Stand in the zone and the flag rises. Leave, and it waits for you with its progress kept. When it reaches the top, a reward crate drops beside it with a smoke signal, and the whole server hears who took it.

This is a PvP event, so you will not be alone.

PvE

Defend the Flag

The PvE answer to King of the Hill. Step into the zone, wait out the arming countdown, and the flag starts to rise. Waves come for you while it does: zombie hordes, boss hordes, armed attackers and bears. You get a warning ten seconds before each wave. If everyone leaves the zone, the flag and the waves pause until someone returns.

Hold out until the flag is up and a reward crate drops beside it, with smoke so everyone can see it. One stronghold at a time is chosen from these:

  • Stark's Castle

  • Pear Plantation

  • Waldoboro Hills Elementary

  • Portland Shipping Yard

  • Warren Cove Cemetery

    The Proving Grounds

The Proving Grounds is rarer and far harder than the rest. It comes at most once between restarts, at a time nobody announces.

PvE

Guarded loot crates

Armed forces dig in at a spot on the map: roadblocks, shipping disruptions, patrols, airfield garrisons. Clear every guard and a crate drops where the last one falls, marked by smoke.

PvE

VIP missions

An important target walks a route across the island with an escort, and the marker moves with them. Take down the VIP and a crate drops where they fell, with a smoke signal.

PvE

Bear hunts

A boss bear stalks the island with other animals around it, and the map marker follows the bear wherever it goes. Bring it down and a crate drops where it falls.

Supply

Airdrops

Supply crates arrive by helicopter. The server announces each drop ahead of time and again when the helicopter is on its way, and a marker shows where it will land. Be quick.

Always out there

Helicopter crashes

Wrecked helicopters lie across the island. Expect infected around the wreck, and loot for anyone who makes it through.
