Twelve years on

STAG's hold on Deer Isle is slipping. Its experiments left the island crawling with things that shouldn't exist. But whispers are spreading across the island: an underground group is organizing to take it back from STAG. You wake up on the shore with no memory. What you do next is up to you.

  • A story to follow

    A main questline that starts at Groot Hill and branches out across the island's factions.

  • Notoriety

    Every job you finish gets you noticed. Factions rank you up, and some only reveal themselves as the story unfolds.

  • Missions that move

    AI patrols, guarded camps, hordes, hunts and loot crates appear across the map.

  • Dinosaurs

    The south-east islands belong to them. The mainland is safer. Mostly.

  • Skills and souls

    Nine skills level up as you use them. Souls carry your progress through death.

  • Traders and markets

    Safe-zone traders and a Grey Market, with rarity tiers from Poor to Mythic to help you judge what is worth carrying.

How to play

  1. Get the DZSA Launcher

    Free from dayzsalauncher.com. It downloads every mod the server needs for you.

  2. Join

    With the launcher installed, click here to join, or search Underground Reborn in the launcher. Joining by hand: 74.50.72.74:2744

  3. Join the Discord

    Rules, wipe dates, events and help all live at discord.gg/tudayz.

The short rules

  • No killing outside PvP zones. The island is PvE. Fights happen in the marked zones. See the map.
  • No base raiding. The exception is the Purge before a wipe, always announced on Discord first.
  • No combat logging. Wait 10 minutes after taking PvP damage before you log out.
  • No cheating, glitching or griefing.

The full rules are on Discord, and they're the ones that count.
